# Rubric: landing (v0.1.0)

Not executed in V1. Ten one-line checks for a human reviewer, derived from cartridge.md.

1. On a 390px phone, the first screen shows what the product is, its price with the financing sentence, and the CTA pill without scrolling past two thumb-scrolls.
2. Word count is 250-450 words.
3. One CTA phrase, identical in the first screen, the closing band and the sticky bar; the only other link is the tenant's secondary text link.
4. Every checked claim pairs what the ad said with a verified fact and cites at least one claim id.
5. The checked claims cover the buyer's logistics the facts support: power, footprint, delivery, assembly, warranty.
6. Price, rating, shipping, warranty term and spec strip are the renderer's, from facts_pack -- no number typed by the writer.
7. The room photo shows the product in a real room, or is absent; never a render.
8. A quote, if present, exists verbatim in facts_pack.reviews_summary.
9. FAQ has 3-5 short questions, answer first, with claim ids where an answer states a number or spec.
10. Boxless: white page, hairlines and white space, one Basalt closing band, 4px radii, no horizontal scroll at 375/390/430px.
