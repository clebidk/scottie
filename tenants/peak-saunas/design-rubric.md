# PEAK design rubric: the brand and the bar (cycle 92 design gate)

Appended by harness/design_gate.py to the engine rubric (harness/design_rubric.md).

## The PEAK brand (the current storefront theme; the old green/cyan mountain mark is retired)

- Page ground white (#FFFFFF). Basalt #161817 for the one dark band, dark pages and the primary button. Ink
  #1A1A1A for text, muted grey (#616161) for secondary text, hairlines rgba(22,24,23,.14).
- ONE accent: brand red #702B34, used sparingly (an accent phrase in the headline, numerals, a 3 px proof bar,
  button hover). No orange, green, cyan, cream, stone or grey panels. No gradients, glows or drop shadows.
- Type: DM Sans for body (400/500, about 16 px, line-height about 1.55); Poppins 600 for headings in sentence
  case; Peak Grotesk Wide (wide, upper case) for display moments; Acid Grotesk is the approved alternative
  display face. No other faces; no all-caps headings outside the display face.
- BOXLESS by default: sections separate by white space and one hairline, not by grey boxes or card borders.
  Any box that remains has a 4 px radius; only pill buttons are fully round (999 px).
- One primary CTA phrase per page, a basalt pill button; at most one quiet secondary text link.
- Real photography of the product in real rooms, and the product cut-outs. Never a stock-photo look,
  filters, or the retired logo.

## The bar: the ten owner-approved reference pages (peak-lp-pack, 2026-10-07)

What "top tier" means, one line each:

1. Ridge listicle: a dark full-bleed photo hero with a huge condensed upper-case display headline; big
   numerals, one photo per reason, a CTA after every second reason; confident and loud, never cluttered.
2. HexClad listicle: the whole page basalt; moody macro photography; five full-width sections, white type,
   one red accent each; very little copy; premium through restraint.
3. Bobbie listicle: a split hero on desktop (photo left, headline + one CTA + three trust facts right),
   photo first on the phone; clear repetition of the CTA after every reason.
4. Caraway listicle: soft and spacious; a short headline, one CTA and one product photo; numbered reasons
   alternating image and copy; generous white space is the design.
5. Venus et Fleur listicle: a luxury-gift hero; check-mark facts; the price printed on the CTA; calm,
   expensive spacing; a plain FAQ.
6. Eight Sleep PDP-style landing: the best high-ticket buy box: a trust bar on top, a gallery with labelled
   hotspots, a tidy buy panel (name, price, rating, model picker, spec bullets, one CTA), a sticky price bar
   on the phone.
7. Oura landing: restraint: one full-bleed dark macro photo, one huge display line, one CTA, then big
   photography with one line each.
8. Function Health landing: a warm cinematic full-bleed hero with a key-stats row inside it; credibility,
   steps, one CTA; red display accents instead of decoration.
9. Rivian landing: a memorable hero: a pill-shaped photo window set between two giant display letters; big
   lifestyle and detail photography; a plain specs row.
10. Plunge landing: a category peer: headline + CTA, a trust row under the hero, a model grid with
    cut-outs, how it works, FAQ; organised, not busy.

Common to all ten: a confident hero composition that owns the first phone screen; generous, deliberate
white space; full-bleed photography used with intent (large, real, cropped well); a clear typographic scale
(display or heading, then body, with visible steps between); one accent colour; no clutter; no generic
card grids; 16 px side gutters on the phone; nothing that looks like a template default.

## PEAK-specific anti-patterns

- Any colour outside the palette above (orange, green, cyan, cream, stone or grey panels), a second accent,
  gradients or drop shadows.
- The retired green/cyan mountain mark, or the old "Peak Saunas" wordmark used as a logo.
- All-caps headings outside the display face; Poppins or DM Sans swapped for another face.
