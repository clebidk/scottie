# Five styles (Caleb, 2026-09-09) — verbatim intent

1. Article / advertorial — magazine piece ("Why backyard heat is replacing the gym commute"). ~70% education, soft Peak CTA at end. Cold Meta. Pair with AEO probes.
2. Simplified product page — one hero SKU, price visible, 3 proof bullets, financing line, one CTA (Buy). Strip nav, reviews, related products. Retargeting + Shopping.
3. Classic long-form landing page — Hero → problem → how it works → specs/proof → social proof → FAQ → sticky CTA. Single goal. Search / high intent.
4. Quiz → personalized result — 4–6 Qs (space, 1–2 person, infrared vs traditional, budget, indoor/outdoor) → named model + why. Multi-SKU fit. (Week 2: interactive, stateful.)
5. Honest comparison / vs page — "Peak vs Clearlight / Sun Valley" or "Infrared vs traditional". Google + late-funnel Meta. Feeds AEO. (Week 2: needs sourced competitor spec table.)

Later: 6. Listicle pre-sell ("7 signs your recovery routine is missing heat") → CTA into quiz or PDP. 7. Consult-first lead LP (calendar embed).

Friday set: 1, 2, 3. All three generated for every ad until 4 and 5 exist.
